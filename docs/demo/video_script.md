# 30-second demo video: script

Screen recording of the app, no voice needed; English subtitles from `subtitles.srt` (burn them
in, or upload the .srt with the video). Default settings of the app: dates 2027-01-01 to
2027-12-31, mast 2 m, default lander (20 kWh battery; values not reviewed by an engineer, marked in
the app).

| Time | Screen and action | Subtitle |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00-0:04.5 | **Site & dates**, Connecting Ridge: the map and the horizon panorama | Will this lander survive at this lunar south-pole site, on these dates? |
| 0:04.5-0:09.5 | Hover over the panorama: grey terrain, orange Sun paths, blue Earth band | Real LOLA terrain horizon; Sun and Earth from NASA SPICE, checked with JPL Horizons. |
| 0:09.5-0:13 | Click **Malapert Massif** on the map; title and statistics change | Pick a site: Malapert Massif, an Artemis III region. |
| 0:13-0:17.5 | Open **Power**: red box *Does not survive* (battery 20 000 Wh, *Battery needed 25,823 Wh*) | Hour by hour: with a 20 kWh battery at 2 m it does not survive 2027. |
| 0:17.5-0:21.5 | In the sidebar move the mast slider to **10**: green *Survives*, *Battery needed 14,128 Wh* | Raise panel and antenna to 10 m: it survives. |
| 0:21.5-0:26 | Open **Mast trade-off**: the four charts and the blue summary | A 10 m mast cuts the battery needed from 25.8 to 14.1 kWh. |
| 0:26-0:30 | Open **Landing windows**: the calendar with the framed best days | Find the best landing days. Lander values: unconfirmed. |

Numbers on screen (Malapert Massif, 2027, default lander): battery needed 25 823 Wh at 2 m and
14 128 Wh at 10 m (docs/VALIDATION.md, T10).

## Recording tips

- Open every page once before recording so that the cached results appear at once.
- Browser window 1920 x 1080, zoom 100 %, light theme; hide bookmarks and other tabs.
- The mast slider in the sidebar has the steps 0, 2, 5, 10 and 20 m; it starts at 2 m.
- No people, faces or voices are needed; the rules forbid any likeness of persons under 18.
- Keep the video at 30 seconds or less and upload it with a public link (for example YouTube,
  unlisted or public); the link goes into the submission form.
